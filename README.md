An original open-world crime game for the browser, set in **Palmira Bay**, a fictional sun-soaked
Gulf-coast city of pastel hotels, glass towers, palm-lined beach boulevards and a working harbor.
Built with three.js (vendored as its minified ES module build) and plain JavaScript modules, no
build step. Every model, texture, sound and note of music is generated by the game's own code.

Rae Castellan is back in town after three years inside. Help Marisol "Mari" Vega save her bar,
the Salt & Lime, from developer and crime boss Lucien Brandt, with mechanic Dez Okafor's help.

## Play

    node serve.ts            # then open http://localhost:8080

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | walk / drive |
| Mouse | look around (click the game to capture the mouse) |
| Shift | sprint |
| Space | jump / handbrake |
| E or F | enter, steal or exit a car |
| Left / right click | punch or shoot / aim |
| Q, wheel, 1-3 | switch weapon |
| R | change radio station |
| H | horn |
| T | look for fares in a Bay Cab |
| Enter | skip dialogue |
| Esc or M | pause menu, map, save and load |

## Features

- A 9 x 8 block city: downtown towers, residential streets with shops and neon, beachfront
  hotels on Coral Parade, Mangrove Park, Sunset Plaza, docks with cranes and containers, a marina,
  a beach with umbrellas and lifeguard towers, a lighthouse, and the sea.
- A 24-hour day/night cycle with golden-hour sunsets, stars, lit windows, street lamps and neon.
- Traffic that follows lanes and turns at junctions; pedestrians who stroll, flee and fall.
- Seven original vehicle models with arcade handling, handbrake drifts, skid marks, tyre smoke,
  damage, fire and explosions; steal any car (drag the driver out) and drive with a chase camera.
- A five-star wanted level: crimes raise it, Bay PD cruisers route through the streets to chase,
  ram and shoot; officers get out to arrest or fight; stay out of sight to lose stars.
- Five story missions with dialogue, markers and objectives (driving, stealing, losing the cops,
  shootouts, pickups, timed deliveries, a heist finale), plus the Sunset Sprint street race,
  Bay Cab taxi fares and twelve hidden Golden Conches.
- Fists, pistol and SMG; health, armour, cash and weapon pickups; FLATLINED and CUFFED respawns.
- HUD with rotating minimap and blips, money, clock, stars, weapon and ammo, health and armour,
  objective panel, speedometer and radio; title screen, pause menu with full map, save/load.
- WebAudio engine, sirens, gunfire, crashes and jingles, and three procedural radio stations.

## Screenshots

`node tools/shots.mjs` (with `node serve.ts --port 8080` running) renders the scenes listed in
`shots.json` from `game/index.html?shot=<name>`. `node tools/playtest.mjs` runs an automated
play-through and reports console errors.

## Layout

`game/src/` holds one module per job: `cityLayout.js` (the plan), `cityMeshes.js` and `props.js`
(geometry), `skyAndWater.js` and `dayNight.js` (lighting), `vehicles.js` and `vehicleModels.js`,
`traffic.js`, `pedestrians.js`, `police.js`, `wanted.js`, `combat.js`, `missions.js`, `story.js`,
`activities.js`, `pickups.js`, `markers.js`, `hud.js`, `minimap.js`, `menus.js`, `save.js`,
`audio.js`, `radio.js`, `effects.js`, `cameraRig.js`, `player.js`, `game.js`, `main.js`, `shots.js`.
